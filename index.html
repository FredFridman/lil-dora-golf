Competition rules

Tournament rules

Individual Championship

72-hole cumulative Net Stableford across Red, Black, Bone Valley and Blue.

0Net double+
1Net bogey
2Net par
3Net birdie
5Net eagle+

Working handicap recommendation: 95% of Course Handicap for the individual competition.

Daily 2-Person Best Ball

Two-person teams compete every championship round. Each player plays his or her own ball; the better net score on each hole counts for the team.

1st team: $300   ·   2nd team: $100 each day.

Working handicap recommendation: 85% of Course Handicap for the team event.

Playing Rules

1
Ready golf. If you are ready and it is safe, hit.
2
Every putt is holed. No gimmes.
3
Clean and place in the fairway. Within 6 inches, no closer to the hole, remaining in the fairway.
4
No mulligans. No breakfast balls.
5
Local rules govern. Streamsong local rules take precedence.

Gross Championship

A separate 72-hole gross title is awarded to the lowest actual stroke total.

To remain eligible for the gross championship, a player must return a complete 72-hole gross score and hole out every hole.

The gross title is secondary to the Lil Dora Net Championship but carries a dedicated purse.

$250 × 16 players

$4,000 tournament purse

The Chain is excluded from the formal purse and handled entirely through cash side bets.
